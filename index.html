The Physics of Chaos – Centrifugal Unbalance

Laundry never distributes perfectly during spin cycle.

At 1400 RPM, a mere 0.5 kg unbalance creates ~2700 N force!

F_u = m_u × r × ω²

m_u: Unbalance mass | r: Drum radius | ω: Angular velocity

3/17

Without Dampers → Chaos

  • Vertical displacement > 20 mm
  • Impact with side panels → loud banging
  • Bearing fatigue failure in <100 cycles
  • Potential water hose rupture

Friction vs. Hydraulic Damper

Friction Type (Coulomb)

• Law: F = μN (Constant force)
• Performance drops when hot. Budget washers.

Hydraulic Type (Viscous)

• Law: F = c·v (Speed proportional)
• Stable up to 120°C. Mid/high-end washers.

7/17

Complete Dynamic Model – FBD

Equation of Motion (Horizontal):

m·ẍ + (c₁+c₂)·ẋ + (k₁+k₂)·x = m_u·r·ω²·sin(ωt)

• m: Tub + laundry mass (20–30 kg)

• k: Spring stiffness (6000–10000 N/m)

• c: Damping coefficient (150–250 N·s/m)

8/17

Step-by-Step Design Calculation

Step 1: ω = (2π·1200)/60 = 125.7 rad/s

Step 2: c_c = 2√(m·k) = 894.4 N·s/m

Step 3: c_total = ζ·c_c = 0.4 × 894.4 = 357.8 N·s/m

Step 4: c_each = 357.8 / 2 = 179 N·s/m

✔ Ideal Hydraulic: Linear (F = c·v)

• Friction Type: Flat horizontal line

Safe Zone: v = 0.1–0.35 m/s

Accessories – The Supporting Cast

  • • Suspension Springs: Supports static weight.
  • • Ball Joints: Allows angular misalignment up to 15°.
  • • Rubber Grommets: Isolates high-frequency noise (>200 Hz).
  • • Concrete Counterweight: Adds mass, lowers system frequency.
11/17

Installation Geometry – The 40° Rule

Why 40 Degrees is optimal?

• 0° (Vertical): No horizontal control; washer walks.

• 90° (Horizontal): No vertical support; spring fatigue.

• 30°–45°: Balanced dual-axis control.

F_vertical = F·sin(40°) | F_horizontal = F·cos(40°)

Failure Modes Diagnostic Chart

Oil Leakage

Wet film → metallic knocking. Seal worn.

Air Ingress

"Squishing" sound. Fluid compresses.

Piston Scratch

High-pitched screech due to debris.

Bushing Wear

"Clunking" at start/stop. Nylon fatigue.
